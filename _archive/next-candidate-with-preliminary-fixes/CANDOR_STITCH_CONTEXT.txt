SECTION 1 — WHAT IS CANDOR
================================================================================

Candor is a web application for job seekers that replaces the manual middle
layer of a job search — the spreadsheets, scattered emails, and notes that
most people currently use to track applications.

Tagline: "Apply with intention, not volume."

The core problem: Job searching is mentally exhausting not because applying
is hard, but because the administrative overhead is enormous. People manage
applications across 5–6 different tools and constantly lose track of where
things stand, what to do next, and why they wanted a role in the first place.
Candor replaces all of that with one place that thinks alongside you.

What makes it different:

1. STORY-DRIVEN, NOT SPREADSHEET-DRIVEN.
   The "My Story" profile — built from your resume and edited by you — powers
   AI email drafting, fit scoring, and narrative. You are not just logging
   applications; you are building a coherent picture of who you are and what
   you want.

2. PUSHES BACK ON LOW-QUALITY APPLICATIONS.
   A required "why this role?" field on every job, a personalisation score on
   every email, and a nudge that says "always edit before sending" are
   deliberate friction points. Candor is explicitly not a spray-and-pray tool.

3. FOLLOWS YOUR PROCESS, NOT A FIXED ONE.
   Every company gets a custom stage pipeline built as you go — not a global
   Kanban board that breaks when one company has 5 rounds and another has 1.

4. CHANNEL-AWARE ACTIVITY LOG.
   Every touchpoint — email, call, portal submission, file sent — is logged in
   one unified place. The AI drafter only activates for written communication.

SECTION 2 — TARGET USER
================================================================================

Primary: Recent graduates and early-career professionals (0–3 years experience)
actively job searching. Applying to 10–50 roles over 3–6 months.

Secondary: Mid-career professionals changing roles.

Profile:
- Applying across multiple companies and industries simultaneously
- Managing a mix of email, phone, and portal-based communications
- Currently tracking in Google Sheets or not at all
- Willing to pay for genuine competitive advantage
- Primarily on desktop during active job search sessions
- Australian market primary, globally applicable

What they care about most:
1. Knowing where things stand without having to remember
2. Not missing follow-ups or opportunities
3. Writing emails that feel authentic, not AI-generated
4. Understanding which applications are worth prioritising
5. Feeling like they are making progress, not just sending into the void

SECTION 3 — DESIGN SYSTEM
================================================================================

AESTHETIC:
- Dark editorial. Not "dark mode SaaS." Think a high-end editorial magazine
  that happens to be an app.
- Warm dark backgrounds (not pure black — slightly warm grey-black)
- Gold as the primary accent colour — not yellow, not orange, muted antique gold
- Minimal colour use — colour carries meaning (status, urgency, success)
- No gradients except for very subtle surface differentiation
- Clean typographic hierarchy with clear serif/sans-serif contrast

TYPOGRAPHY:
- Display / headings / company names / brand: Instrument Serif (Google Fonts)
  - Used for: the "Candor" logo, company names in detail view, page titles,
    the My Story narrative, section headers like "Applications"
- Body / UI / labels / buttons: DM Sans (Google Fonts)
  - Used for: everything else — inputs, labels, badges, body text

COLOUR TOKENS (CSS custom properties):

  Backgrounds / Surfaces:
  --bg:    #111114   (page background — very dark warm near-black)
  --s1:    #17171b   (secondary surfaces — slightly lifted)
  --s2:    #1e1e22   (inputs, card backgrounds)
  --s3:    #252529   (active/hover states)
  --s4:    #2c2c31   (hover on already-dark elements)

  Text:
  --t1:    #edeae3   (primary text — warm near-white)
  --t2:    #9b98a4   (secondary text — mid-grey)
  --t3:    #635f6e   (placeholder / dim / tertiary)

  Accent — Gold:
  --gold:  #c9aa7e   (primary accent — antique gold)
  --gold2: #e3c99a   (gold hover state — brighter)
  --gold3: #f1dfc0   (light gold — very subtle tints)

  Status colours (each has a solid and a 13% opacity "dim" variant):
  --green:  #4ea375   (offer, success, complete)

BORDER RADIUS RULES:
- Page/section containers: 10–13px
- Cards and input fields: 7–10px
- Small chips/badges: 6–7px
- Buttons that are wider: 8px
- Pill shapes (status badges, filter pills, small tags): 100px
- Icon buttons (square): 7–8px

SPACING:
- Nav height: 52–54px
- Standard padding inside cards: 12–16px
- Gap between stacked sections: 16–20px
- Inline element gap: 6–10px

ANIMATIONS (all CSS keyframes):
- `up`: opacity 0→1 + translateY(10px→0) — page/section entrance
- `fade`: opacity 0→1 — simple fade
- `slidein`: opacity 0→1 + translateX(16px→0) — detail panel slide from right
- `slidedown`: translateY(-8px→0) — dropdowns
- `spin`: full 360 rotation — loading spinners
- `gw`: width 0→100% — progress bar fill animation
- `pulse`: box-shadow 0→gold glow pulse — active state on logo dot
- `breathe`: opacity 0.5→1 pulse — subtle breathing effect
- `dropdown-in`: scale(0.95→1) + translateY(-4px→0) — dropdown menus
- `modal-in`: scale(0.96→1) + translateY(8px→0) + opacity — modal entrance

MICRO-INTERACTIONS:
- Every button has a hover state: background lift, color shift, or translateY(-1px)
- Active/pressed states: translateY(0) or background darken
- All transitions: 0.12–0.2s ease
- Destructive hover (delete, reject): turns red/rdd on hover
- Gold buttons: lighten to --gold2 on hover
- Ghost buttons: border brightens, text lightens on hover
- Icon buttons: scale(1.1) on hover, scale(0.95) on press

SECTION 4 — APPLICATION STRUCTURE & PAGES
================================================================================

The app has these routes:

  /            → redirects to /dashboard (if authed) or /login
  /login       → login page
  /signup      → signup page
  /auth/callback → Supabase email confirmation handler
  /dashboard   → main application (table + detail)
  /story       → My Story profile page

ROUTING LOGIC:
- Unauthenticated users trying to access /dashboard or /story → redirect to /login
- Authenticated users hitting /login or /signup → redirect to /dashboard

SECTION 20 — AI FEATURES
================================================================================

AI FEATURES SUMMARY:

1. EMAIL / COVER LETTER GENERATION (streaming):
   Route: POST /api/ai/generate-email
   Input: company, role, status/stage, tone, why, user narrative
   Output: streaming text displayed with typewriter animation
   System prompt rules: under 200 words, no clichés, first person,
   clear next step, leave personalisation placeholders

2. JD ANALYSIS (non-streaming):
   Route: POST /api/ai/analyse-jd
   Input: job description text, company, role, user skills
   Output: { fitScore: 0–100, matched: string[], gaps: string[], angle: string }
   Result stored on job.fit and shown in Overview tab

3. URL JOB IMPORT (non-streaming):
   Route: POST /api/import-url
   Input: { url: string }
   Output: { company, role, location, salary } — all nullable
   Pre-fills Add Job modal
   LinkedIn blocks server-side fetch — graceful fallback to manual entry

4. MY STORY AI REFRESH:
   Route: POST /api/ai/refresh-story (or similar)
   Input: user's timeline entries, skills, current narrative
   Output: updated narrative paragraph + 6 observation cards
   Regenerates on "Refresh AI" button click in My Story page

5. AI INSIGHTS STRIP:
   Contextual insights shown in Activity tab right column
   6 insight cards generated based on company + role + user profile
   Types: ANGLE, TONE, MATCH, HOOK, LEVERAGE, TIMING
   Quick edit buttons trigger refinement prompts

PERSONALISATION SCORE (client-side, no AI):
  Calculated on email body text:
  - "Low": company name not mentioned in body → red badge
  - "Good": company name mentioned → amber badge
  - "Strong": company mentioned AND body > 280 chars → green badge
  Always shown below the email drafter body area

SECTION 26 — COPY & VOICE
================================================================================

The product voice is calm, direct, and editorial. Not bubbly or over-excited.
No exclamation marks in UI copy unless in toast confirmations.
Use sentence case for labels (not Title Case everywhere).
Italic serif for supportive/contextual copy.

Key copy examples:

  Button: "Draft now" not "Start Drafting!"
  Placeholder: "Add why this role matters to you..." (lowercase start)
  Empty state: "No applications yet — paste a URL above or click Add Job."
  Nudge: "Always edit before sending — your voice matters more than the AI's."
  Next action: "X days since you applied — time to send a follow-up."
  Checklist empty: "No checklist items yet.\nAdd what matters to you."
  Loading: "Signing you in..." (with trailing ellipsis, no exclamation)
  Gmail: "Gmail connected ✓" (checkmark, not exclamation)
  Delete confirm: "Delete this note?" (question, not warning)

Section labels are ALL CAPS, 9px, letter-spacing 0.1em:
  "NOTES" "NEXT ACTION" "WHY THIS ROLE" "STATUS" "INSIGHTS"
