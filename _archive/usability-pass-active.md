## AI opportunities grounded in this pass (proposals, not implemented services)

1. **Evidence-to-variant review.** The user currently chooses each summary/bullet manually and must understand why a master edit matters. Given an explicitly selected job ad and selected master evidence, suggest a small set of relevant bullets with exact source references and show unsupported requirements separately. Produce proposed selections/wording for review, never overwrite master or submitted copies. Benefit hypothesis: less time checking which experiences apply. Measure time to a reviewed variant, percentage of suggestions accepted after correction, and unsupported claims caught in review—not interview uplift. Minimum data: one job ad, selected evidence items, provenance, employer AI rules. Local/manual fallback: theme selection and side-by-side source check. Medium implementation effort; provider/data consent required, confidential client material excluded unless deliberately selected.
2. **Reviewed résumé intake.** Empty-master users hit a substantial entry task even after adding the missing controls. Parse a user-selected plain-text résumé into proposed roles, education and bullets, each quoting its source span. Display an import review/diff before saving; leave dates and achievements unresolved when absent. Do not infer goals, skill percentages or impact. Benefit hypothesis: less transcription and fewer missed sections. Measure reviewed import completion time and corrected fields. Minimum data: that résumé only, no vault upload. Manual fallback: the new master forms; deterministic parsing first. Medium effort. Never present speculative rewriting as extracted fact.
3. **Spreadsheet column mapping, mostly deterministic.** The observed CSV paste was rejected. Add a preview mapping common company/role/stage/date/link columns with duplicate warnings and separate merge/replace behaviour. AI would only help interpret unfamiliar headers or propose status mappings; deterministic parsing does the actual import. Closed listing vs rejected must remain explicit. Benefit hypothesis: bringing an existing pipeline requires less re-entry. Measure imported rows accepted/corrected and duplicate/date errors, not engagement. Minimum data: chosen rows/columns; strip contact notes unless deliberately included. Local manual mapping is both fallback and likely first implementation. Small-to-medium effort before any AI service.

Retention should come from recovering saved context, seeing the next useful action, and reusing verified evidence. No streak pressure, fabricated fit scores or bulk outreach incentives are needed.
