# Proposed AI assistance for Candor

8 September 2026. This ranking combines three independent, hands-on agent reviews: [first-time applicant](_archive/usability-pass-first.md), [busy employed applicant](_archive/usability-pass-busy.md), and [active applicant with an existing pipeline](_archive/usability-pass-active.md). They used synthetic workspaces and actual browser interactions. These are product hypotheses, not findings from recruited users or evidence of hiring improvement.

The current app uses deterministic helpers and local editing. Its research control prepares a brief; it does not find people or connect to LinkedIn. Basic usability defects identified in the reviews have received local fixes. AI should now be considered where it could reduce repeated interpretation or transcription, with a reviewable result and the manual workflow always available. The ranking below reflects observed friction, dependencies and implementation risk.

## Ranked opportunities

| Rank and proposal | Observed reason to prioritise | Minimum input and reviewable result | Sources, privacy and manual fallback | Effort and useful progress to measure |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **1. Reviewed job-ad intake** | The first applicant arrived with a URL; the old parser treated it as full ad text. Accurate role context also underpins tailoring and contact research. The local URL/full-text distinction is now fixed. | One pasted ad and its source/date, or a page fetch separately authorised by the user. Propose company, title, location, responsibilities and requirements, with supporting text spans and unanswered questions. Preserve the original. | A URL is not an ad; extraction does not verify current availability. Treat retrieved pages as untrusted data. Send no résumé/contact notes for this step. Manual fields and current local text extraction remain available. | **Medium**, starting with pasted text. Measure completion of an accurately reviewed opportunity, corrected/missing fields, and whether the person can choose a useful next step. |
| **2. Evidence-grounded résumé and variant assistance** | Both first and active applicants hit empty-master entry friction. The active applicant then selected evidence manually and had to interpret master changes. Entry controls and version safeguards now exist; transcription and relevance decisions remain. | A user-selected résumé or evidence entries, their sources, one selected ad and applicable employer AI rules. First offer reviewed extraction into the master; separately suggest relevant evidence and proposed variant wording. Show a diff and source for each material claim. | Do not infer dates, titles, achievements or measured impact. Keep personal/team contribution distinct and retain rejected-claim corrections. Use only selected evidence; exclude client-confidential material by default. Archive accepted draft changes and preserve submitted snapshots. Manual entry, themes and source checking remain usable. | **Medium to high**, staged as intake then tailoring. Measure reviewed intake/variant completion, corrected fields, defensibility of accepted claims, and successful recovery of an earlier or submitted copy. |
| **3. Selected conversation to context, optional action and draft** | The busy applicant reconstructed a reply, uncertain team context, follow-up and next question across several fields. The old outline also replaced a personal draft; preservation has since been fixed. | Only the selected conversation excerpt, relevant role/contact context and an existing draft if chosen. Suggest a concise notes update, optional linked task or a revision to the draft. Quote the excerpt behind proposed facts and let the user accept changes individually. | Conversation text can contain another person's private details. Show the intended payload, omit unrelated messages, and leave uncertain commitments/dates unresolved. Never invent familiarity, replace a draft silently, mark it sent or send it. Existing notepad, shared task records, contact editor and draft editor are the fallback. | **Medium** after provider choice and a review interface. Measure whether a user can resume the intended conversation without reconstructing it, factual corrections, preservation of intended meaning and recovery of prior wording. |
| **4. Source-cited contact research** | First and busy applicants deliberately recorded people whose identity, team or hiring responsibility was unverified. More names alone would not resolve their uncertainty. This also has greater retrieval and access complexity than the earlier proposals. | Reviewed company/title/location/ad context and a specific research question. Suggest relevant public team members, programme contacts or possible hiring managers with URLs, dates checked, relevance and uncertainty. | Separate a suggestion from verified hiring responsibility. Verify identity, employer and current role; no guessed emails or referral claims. Mutual connections require separately authorised LinkedIn access. Private notes are excluded by default; no automatic outreach. The current local brief, manual research and contact entry remain available. | **High** for retrieval, identity checks and stale-source handling. Measure whether cited sources answer the user's question, identity/relevance errors and how many suggestions remain supportable when rechecked. |

A separate active-applicant finding was unsupported CSV import. A local column-mapping preview with duplicate and status/date checks is the appropriate first response. AI interpretation of unusual headers is optional later; it should not become a prerequisite for importing a spreadsheet. Likewise, event-to-contact-status rules, autosave and shared checklist links are deterministic behaviour.

## Decision before any connected trial

Choose the provider and model, authentication method, permitted data destinations, retention/training policy and a bounded spend limit. Decide separately whether public-page retrieval and LinkedIn access are authorised. Show which selected data will leave the browser; the full workspace, vault, mailbox and private contact records are not default inputs. No provider installation, paid request or external integration is authorised by this document.

## Small evaluation plan

1. Build a small, sanitised case set from the three reviewed journeys: a bare URL, an incomplete ad, a résumé with uncertain dates/team results, a master correction, a tentative reply and similarly named people with stale public profiles. Keep expected facts and prohibited inferences alongside each case.
2. Record a manual baseline using the existing app. After the decisions above, test one proposed assistance step at a time on the same cases. Review source accuracy, unsupported claims, correction effort and whether the intended task finishes; report actual observations without invented uplift.
3. Verify keep/accept/reject controls, clipboard/provider failures, data minimisation, draft recovery and submitted-copy immutability. Missing context should prompt a focused question or leave the field unresolved. A failed or rejected suggestion must leave manual work intact.
4. Prioritise a small opt-in user trial only if the assistance proves useful and reliable in those checks. Judge repeat use by whether people recover context and complete worthwhile work, without optimising application counts, messages, streaks, fit scores or predicted interviews.

See [PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md](PRODUCT-CONTEXT.md) for the current product direction. This document proposes an evaluation sequence; it does not claim that connected AI has been built or validated.
